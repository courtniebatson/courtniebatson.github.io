About

Over 14 years, I've moved from front-line program coordination into director-level leadership across three organizations, then chosen to specialize full-time in training and development. Along the way, I've run programs for foster youth and led how staff are trained for expanded learning programs. Since 2021, I've also built a second track leading community engagement and DEI strategy for Black-owned food and beverage businesses. In both, I start by finding the real gap, then design training people can put to use right away.

What I do

Training & Development

I assess what teams need, then design and lead workshops that help staff feel confident in the classroom and in expanded learning programs.

Community Engagement

I build partnerships with schools, agencies, and businesses, and lead strategy sessions that move equity and inclusion work forward.

Student Enrichment

I've led youth programs from middle school through young adulthood, including housing, mentoring, and employment readiness for foster youth.

How I work

1

Discover

I start with a real needs assessment — what staff, students, or partners actually need, not what's assumed.

2

Design

I build the training, curriculum, or partnership plan that fits that specific group.

3

Deliver & iterate

I facilitate it live, then fold feedback and outcomes back into the next version.
